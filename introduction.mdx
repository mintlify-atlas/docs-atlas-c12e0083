## Welcome to Requiem for Earth

**Requiem for Earth** (Requiem por la Tierra) is a powerful environmental art exhibition hosted by COBATAB Plantel #40. This immersive experience features sculptures representing endangered species, designed to inspire action and awareness about biodiversity conservation.

<Note type="info">
  **Exhibition Date:** May 30, 12:00 p.m.  
  **Location:** COBATAB Plantel #40  
  **Format:** Sculpture exhibition with digital pass access
</Note>

### Why This Exhibition Matters

Our planet faces unprecedented environmental challenges. Through art and education, *Requiem for Earth* brings the beauty and struggle of endangered animals into focus, creating an emotional connection that drives meaningful change.
