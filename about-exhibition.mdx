# About Requiem for Earth

**Requiem for Earth** (Requiem por la Tierra) is an environmental art exhibition that combines artistic expression with urgent conservation messaging. Hosted by COBATAB Plantel #40, this exhibition transforms education into experience through powerful sculptures of endangered species.

## The Importance of Conservation

Our planet is at a critical juncture. The exhibition addresses urgent environmental challenges:

- **Habitat Loss**: Deforestation, urbanization, and climate change threaten ecosystems worldwide
- **Species Extinction**: Hundreds of species face extinction, disrupting ecological balance
- **Climate Crisis**: Rising temperatures and extreme weather patterns endanger wildlife
- **Human Impact**: Understanding our role in both causing and solving environmental problems

<Note type="warning">
  The sculptures in this exhibition represent real animals facing real threats. Each piece tells a story of resilience—and serves as a call to action.
</Note>

## Exhibition Philosophy

Art has the unique power to communicate complex environmental issues in ways that data alone cannot. *Requiem for Earth* leverages this power through:

### Emotional Engagement

Sculptures create visceral connections that statistics cannot achieve. By seeing the beauty and vulnerability of endangered species rendered in three dimensions, visitors develop personal stakes in conservation outcomes.

## About COBATAB Plantel #40

COBATAB (Colegio de Bachilleres de Tabasco) Plantel #40 is an educational institution committed to holistic student development. By hosting *Requiem for Earth*, the school demonstrates its dedication to:

- **Environmental Education**: Integrating conservation into curriculum
- **Cultural Enrichment**: Exposing students to meaningful artistic experiences
- **Community Leadership**: Serving as a hub for important social conversations
- **Student Empowerment**: Inspiring young people to become environmental advocates
